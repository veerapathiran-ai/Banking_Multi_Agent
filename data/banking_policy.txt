SAVINGS ACCOUNT

A savings account is intended for personal deposits
and withdrawals.

MINIMUM BALANCE

Minimum balance requirements depend on the account
product and bank. Customers should check the official
schedule of charges.

LOAN ELIGIBILITY

Loan eligibility may depend on verified income,
credit history, existing obligations, employment
details and bank underwriting rules.

LOAN INTEREST AND EMI

Interest rates, fees and EMI amounts depend on the
lender, product, tenure and customer profile.

FRAUD AND DISPUTES

Customers should promptly contact their bank through
an official channel if they notice an unknown or
unauthorized transaction.

Never share your PIN, password, CVV or OTP.

TRANSACTION STATUS

A transaction marked successful in the sample data
is a demonstration record only.

A flagged transaction is sent for review by the
sample fraud-screening agent.


KYC POLICY

KYC stands for Know Your Customer.

Banks verify customer identity before opening
bank accounts or providing financial services.

CUSTOMER IDENTIFICATION

Customers may be required to provide valid
identity proof and address proof.

IDENTITY VERIFICATION

Banks verify customer information using
approved identity verification procedures.

CUSTOMER DOCUMENTS

Commonly accepted documents may include
government-issued identity documents and
address verification documents.

CUSTOMER MONITORING

Banks monitor customer accounts and transactions
to identify suspicious activities.

KYC COMPLIANCE

Banks follow applicable KYC and Anti-Money
Laundering regulations.

CUSTOMER INFORMATION UPDATE

Customers should update their identity and
address information whenever required.
